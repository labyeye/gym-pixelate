NestGym

Complete gym management software — front desk, members, training, billing, reports and member portal in one platform.

Product report · Prepared by Pixelate Nest · October 2026

1. Overview

NestGym is a cloud (SaaS) platform that runs the daily operations of a gym or fitness chain: selling and renewing memberships, controlling entry, managing trainers and classes, billing with GST, selling products, tracking expenses and giving the owner clear business numbers. Members get their own mobile-friendly portal.

4. Key features in detail

Membership management

  • Flexible plans: duration, price, admission fee, GST mode, inclusions, access hours, freeze / transfer rules, grace period
  • Sell, renew (queued after the running plan so no days are lost), upgrade (unused days credited), freeze / unfreeze (end date extended), cancel with refund, transfer to another person
  • Automatic hourly expiry processing
  • Full history kept — renewals never overwrite old records

Entry control & attendance

  • One rule-set for desk, QR/manual and face check-in: expired, dues pending, frozen, outside plan hours, already inside
  • Manager override with a mandatory reason, recorded in the audit trail
  • Face-recognition kiosk (AI service) with member face enrolment from the profile
  • Visit duration and auto check-out

Billing & money

  • Itemised GST invoices with discount and coupon, running invoice numbers per gym (never reused)
  • Part-payments and dues; every payment and refund is an append-only ledger entry, so the books always reconcile
  • Printable invoice with GSTIN, address and footer
  • Safe against double-clicks and retries — a payment is never counted twice

Sales growth

  • Enquiry pipeline with notes, follow-up dates, free trial and one-click conversion
  • Renewal worklist with one-tap WhatsApp message links, expiring-soon and at-risk (lapsing) members, birthdays
  • Coupons and offers

Training

  • Group classes with capacity, waitlist auto-promotion and monthly class credits
  • Personal-training packages with session scheduling, trainer-clash guard and per-session trainer commission
  • Workout and diet plan builders, reusable templates, body-measurement tracking

Operations

  • Lockers, equipment service tracker, announcements
  • Shop / POS with stock control; expenses and profit & loss
  • Staff attendance and payroll; multi-branch with per-branch staff
  • Activity / audit log of important actions

Security

  • Every gym's data is isolated at the database layer — one gym can never read another's records
  • Role-based permissions, role ceilings (a manager cannot create an owner), login lock-out protection
  • Payment secrets stored encrypted; payments verified server-side with the gateway

5. Member portal

Members sign in on the normal login page and get a mobile-first portal. The front desk creates the login from the member profile (with a ready WhatsApp message containing the credentials) and can reset or disable it any time.

  • Membership status and days left
  • Bills and online payment through the gym's own Razorpay account
  • Class booking and cancellation, with waitlist
  • Trainer, personal training and locker details
  • Workout and diet plan, self-logged weigh-ins, announcements

A member login can only reach the member's own records and is blocked from every staff screen.
